## Who makes Honeychrome

Honeychrome is part of the [Cytkit](https://cytkit.com) project to build a complete open-source spectral cytometer. Its main developers are Samson Rogers (Astute Devices), Oliver Burton (University of Cambridge), who developed AutoSpectral, and Angxiao Lu.

Thanks to Joan Escriva Font for faster loading of large files, to Paul Heisig (Amsterdam UMC) and David Novo for the work behind the noise model, and to Catherine Vant, Lotte Carr, Scott Tasker, Philip Jones, Robyn Pritchard and everyone who tested pre-release builds.

***

## Built on open source

Honeychrome builds on [FlowKit](https://github.com/whitews/flowkit) for gates, transforms and FCS input and output, [PySide6](https://pypi.org/project/PySide6/) (Qt for Python) for the interface, [pyqtgraph](https://www.pyqtgraph.org/) for fast interactive plots, NumPy and scikit-learn for analysis, and Matplotlib and Seaborn for graphics.

Found a bug or have an idea? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Cytkit/honeychrome/issues). Contributions are welcome.
