- Start from your Honeychrome gates or import a **FlowJo workspace**.
- Each gate becomes a data-driven algorithm, trained on samples you choose. Keep a gate fixed or recalculate it per sample, and nudge thresholds stricter or looser.
- Population frequencies, group statistics with heatmaps and volcano plots, sample PCA, predictive analysis and Flow Set Enrichment Analysis.
