- Histograms, 2D density and dot plots coloured by gate, ribbon plots and logicle transforms you can drag.
- Rectangle, polygon, ellipse, quadrant and range gates on a shared gating template.
- **Per-sample gating:** customise a gate for one sample with a right click. Only the gates you change are stored, and every tab uses them.
